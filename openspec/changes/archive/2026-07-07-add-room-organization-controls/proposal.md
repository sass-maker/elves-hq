# Add Room Organization Controls

## Why

The room deck can page through rooms, but once several products and rooms exist the founder still has to visually hunt for the right room. The V0 brief explicitly calls out organized rooms. The cockpit should make it easy to focus on rooms by signal and sort order without adding a heavier board or more visible panels.

## What Changes

- Add compact room-deck organization controls.
- Let the founder filter the deck by room signal: all, needs me, working, ready, failed, blocked, or idle/done.
- Let the founder sort rooms by priority signal, newest activity, or project name.
- Keep the existing active/all scope, paging, and selected room detail behavior.

## Out Of Scope

- New daemon endpoints or persistence.
- Drag-and-drop room ordering.
- Kanban lanes.
- Multi-select bulk actions.
- Cloud sync or hosted state.
