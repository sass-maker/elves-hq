# Filter Room Deck

## Why

Done rooms are useful history, but they should not compete with active work in the default task-room deck. The cockpit should keep the working surface quiet by showing active rooms first, while still letting the founder inspect all rooms when needed.

## What Changes

- Add an Active/All room deck filter in the task-room pane.
- Default the deck to Active, hiding `done` rooms from the card deck.
- Keep the selected room detail stable when possible.
- Let the founder switch to All to inspect completed or rejected rooms.

## Out Of Scope

- Drag-and-drop room ordering.
- Custom saved filters.
- Kanban columns.
- Deleting or archiving rooms.
